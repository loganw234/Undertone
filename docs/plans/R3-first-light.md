## The first build

The build's maxima are parameters of the RTL (L5). **What a build cannot run, its capability registers say, and a blob past them is refused by name**: that is D0's contract, and the first build is where it is first held in fabric.

- **It is small, on purpose** (question 2). The lead's proposal: 4 filters of one row each, 128 taps, the definition's twelve hidden units, 16-bit one-channel samples; the definition unchanged. That is about 724 DSP slices by arithmetic (L18): under a third of what L17 gives SLR0, and still inside it were that SURVEY figure twice the truth, so **under 16-bit samples no part of the decision rests on it**. Under 24-bit three-channel samples, question 3's alternative, the same four filters, each on all three channels, are 12 rows and about 1,796 slices (L18a), three quarters of L17's figure, and its bank would wait on wave 0's measurement. A hidden unit sums four products where at the definition's maxima it sums sixteen, and the hidden layer is still all there. The definition's maxima on the card are a later build's: by L16 they are 2,672 slices for the bank and the head alone, which L17's unmeasured figure puts past one SLR. The head's cost at that size stays the open question it was at round 2's close.
- **Its clock** is asked as 100 MHz: a DECISION of the lead's, for a first light that is about bit identity and not speed. The build's timing report gives the slack at that clock. The likeliest place it fails is not the head: a row's 128 products are summed in one stage (`docs/rtl/core.md`, limit 2). If the build misses, its worst paths are read and recorded, the round ends at the build, and a core with more stages is a parcel of a later round. Asking for 10 MHz, the trap of L25, would show nothing.
- **What the first build is not:** the definition's maxima, a second sample format, the scorer, replay from HBM as the source, one record a clock (L12). Each is a later build's.
