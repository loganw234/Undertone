# PROVENANCE: what this project has read from outside, and on what terms

Started by the parcel LCH on 2026-11-23, with [study D1](docs/studies/D1-launch-data.md), and brought up to date the same day after its first look at real records, [study D1b](docs/studies/D1b-first-look.md). One row per source: who publishes it, its terms as they were read, and what this project does with it.

**What is in this repository from any of them:** short attributed quotations, each of 25 words or fewer; four launch-site coordinates; and, for the three data services marked *used* below, rows of station metadata (a station's code, its coordinates, its channels and their sample rates). After the owner allowed downloads on 2026-11-23, 43 requests were made for study D1b: waveforms, three catalogue files and listings, 38 files in all. **None of them is committed.** Each is listed with its request, time, size and SHA-256 in [manifest.tsv](docs/studies/d1/manifest.tsv), and two small figures drawn from the waveforms are under `docs/studies/d1/fig/`. Software packages were installed into a virtual environment outside the repository and nowhere else. No PDF has been fetched. No page and no service answer is committed.

**How a quotation here is held.** Every text between straight double quotes in this file is listed in [quotes.tsv](docs/studies/d1/quotes.tsv), and [check_quotes.py](docs/studies/d1/check_quotes.py) holds it, letter for letter, against the copy of the page saved on the day it was read. Terms are given *as read*: a locator, a date and the words. Where no terms were read, the row says so. Nothing here is legal advice, and a page read on one day can say something else on another.

**Accepting terms is the owner's to do.** Two services say that using them accepts terms. They were queried before those pages were read (study D1, row M8), and both were held until the owner ruled. On 2026-11-23 Logan allowed EarthScope; its rows are committed from then on, with the acknowledgement its terms ask for. Raspberry Shake is still *held*: its answers stay outside the repository, no row from them is committed, and nothing further is asked of it.

## Adding a source

A later parcel that reads a new source adds a row here in the same commit: the publisher, the terms as read with a locator and a date, and the use. If a service or a site says that using it accepts terms, the parcel stops before its first request and asks the lead; the owner accepts terms, not an agent.
